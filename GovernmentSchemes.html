Government Schemes

Explore schemes and subsidies available for farmers

PM-KISAN

Financial assistance of ₹6000/year directly to farmers' bank accounts

  • Eligibility: All landholding farmers
  • Benefits: ₹2000 every 4 months

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana

Crop insurance scheme providing financial support in case of crop failure

  • Eligibility: All farmers growing notified crops
  • Benefits: Up to 90% premium subsidy

Soil Health Card Scheme

Free soil testing and nutrient status assessment for better farming

  • Eligibility: All farmers
  • Benefits: Free soil health card

Kisan Credit Card

Affordable credit for agriculture and allied activities

  • Eligibility: Farmers, tenant farmers, sharecroppers
  • Benefits: Low-interest loans up to ₹3 lakhs
